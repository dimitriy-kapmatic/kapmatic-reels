Pet groomers: a caller asks one thing, "How much is a groom?" If you only say a number, the call ends there.

1. First, ask about their dog.
2. Then give your price for that groom.
3. And offer a time: "When would you like to come in?"

The goal of the call is the booking. The prices on screen are a made-up example.
