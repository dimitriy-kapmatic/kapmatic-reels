Groomers: someone calls and asks how much. Don't just say a number, or the call ends there.

1. First, ask about their dog.
2. Then give your price for that groom.
3. And offer a time right away: "I have Thursday at 10. Want it?"

The goal of the call is the booking. The prices on screen are a made-up example.
