Tattoo shop and spa owners: a missed call is a customer who may call the next shop. Send one text within the hour.

1. Say who you are: "Hi, it's [name] at [shop]."
2. Say you saw the call: "Sorry I missed your call."
3. Ask one question: "What can I help you with?"

Save it as a reply on your phone so it takes ten seconds.
