Barbershop owners: raising your prices in January? Steal this message (a made-up example): "Heads up, Marcus: from Jan 2 a cut is $38."

1. No long apology. One clear line.
2. Say the new price and the day it starts.
3. Give anyone with a booking thirty days' notice.
4. Say it once, in person or by email, then stop explaining.

Clients expect prices to change now and then. Only text clients who agreed to get texts from you.
