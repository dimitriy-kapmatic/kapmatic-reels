Barbers: raising prices in January? Send this first. A made-up example: "Heads up, Marcus: from Jan 2, a cut is $38. Your Jan 9 booking stays at $35. See you then!"

1. No long apology. One clear line.
2. The new price, and the day it starts.
3. Anyone already booked keeps the old price.
4. Say it once, then stop explaining.

Clients expect prices to change now and then. Only text clients who agreed to get texts from you.
