Gym owners: members who never take a class cancel more. In a UK study by The Retention People and Dr. Melvyn Hillsdon (2013-14 data), gym-only members had a 56% higher risk of canceling than members who take group classes. After adjusting for other differences between the two groups, it was still 26% (reported in Health Club Management and by Les Mills, which sells group fitness programs).

1. Pick ten members who never take a class.
2. Invite each one by name, to one class.
3. Name the day, the time and the coach.
