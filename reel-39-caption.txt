Gym owners: class members stay, gym-only members leave. In a survey of 10,000 members in England by The Retention People, gym-only members were 56% more likely to cancel than members who take group classes (reported in a Club Industry article sponsored by Les Mills).

1. Pick ten members who never take a class.
2. Invite each one in person, by name.
3. Name the class, the day and the coach.
