Vet clinic owners: dentists book your next visit before you leave. Vets can too. It is called forward booking, and the AAHA and AVMA describe it as scheduling the next appointment before the client leaves the practice.

1. At checkout, don't ask if they want to come back.
2. Offer a date: same time, when the next checkup is due.
3. Write it on a card before they walk out.

It is easier to say yes now than to call later. The clinic on screen is a made-up example.
