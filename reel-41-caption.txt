Dental office owners and managers: what does a new patient hear from you after the first visit? The American Dental Association suggests a personal welcome letter after the first appointment.

1. Send a short note the same week.
2. Use their name, and say you are glad they came.
3. Tell them where to find forms and hours on your website.
4. Sign it from a person, not the practice.
