Dentists: what does a new patient hear after the first visit? Usually, nothing. The American Dental Association suggests a personal welcome letter after the first appointment.

1. Send a short note the same week.
2. Their name, a thank-you, and how to reach you.
3. Sign it from a person, not the practice.
